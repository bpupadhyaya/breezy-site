Tilebrook

Slide & Match

Match glazed ceramic tiles that can see each other. Then the brook flows and the whole board slides. Every match changes what is next to what, so every move is a small plan.

1. MatchTap two tiles with the same glaze that sit in one row or column with only water between them.
2. SlideThe brook flows and every tile drifts until it meets an edge, a mossy stone or another tile.
3. PlanSome brooks turn after each match; some you steer yourself. Clear the board to fire a new piece for your shelf.

What is in it

  • 120 generated stages across ten bends of the brook, each checked to be solvable.
  • A new Daily Board every day, the same for everyone, with a streak.
  • Endless mode: chase your best score as the boards grow.
  • A kiln shelf of 36 ceramic pieces to fire, in three glazes.
  • Optional video hints and re-glaze; undo is always free in stages.
  • Works offline. Calm motion option, haptics and sound switches.
  • Free with ads; one purchase removes the full-screen ads.
