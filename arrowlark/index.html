How it plays

Two rules, understood in the first level. Every board is built so that it can be cleared.

1

Follow the ribbon

The ribbon at the top shows which colour leaves next. Only that colour is awake.

2

Keep the lane clear

An arrow slides straight out of its head. Another arrow in the way makes it bump back.

3

Find the order

Which of two same-coloured arrows goes first? The wrong pick can leave you stuck. Rewind and think again.

Ways to play

Levels

180 boards in 12 flights, growing from a tiny tutorial to big, tangled beds of arrows. Earn up to three stars on each.

Daily

One new board each day, the same for everyone. Keep your streak going.

Flock Run

An endless run of fresh boards with three hearts. Watch an optional video to fly on.

Calm and clear

No timers. Free rewinds. Optional shape marks help tell the colours apart.

Collect the flock

Stars and daily boards earn feathers. Every few feathers hatch a new sticker: 36 larks in six colours and six accessories. Perch your favourite on the home screen.
