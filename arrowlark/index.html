How it plays

Two rules, understood in the first level. Boards are generated so that each one can be cleared.

1

Follow the ribbon

The ribbon shows which colour leaves next. Only that colour is awake and bright.

2

Keep the lane clear

An arrow slides straight out of its head. If another arrow is in the way, it bumps back.

3

Find the order

Pick wrong and you can get stuck. Rewind is free, so think again and earn three stars.

Ways to play

No timers, ever.

Levels

180 boards in 12 flights, from a tiny tutorial to big tangled beds of arrows.

Daily

One new board each day, the same for everyone. Keep your streak going.

Flock Run

An endless run of fresh boards with three hearts.

Calm and clear

Free rewinds, optional shape marks for colours, and a calm-motion switch.

Collect the flock

Stars and daily boards earn feathers. Every few feathers hatch a new sticker: 36 larks in six colours and six accessories. Perch your favourite on the home screen.
