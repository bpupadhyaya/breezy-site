Paper Folds

Fold a patterned sheet along its creases. Corners show the other side. When the last fold lands, a small picture appears, and a vermilion seal goes on it.

Learn it in one fold.

The very first sheet teaches itself: a dashed crease, a drifting finger, one gentle drag.

Read the sheet

Every square has a colour, and a small corner showing what is on its other side.

2

Drag across a crease

The paper bends, creases and snaps down. Fold over the front, or tuck behind.

3

Reveal the picture

Match the model in as few folds as you can. Three stars for par.

A reason to come back.

All sheets are generated by the game and checked by a solver, so every one can be folded.

Journey

48 sheets in six chapters, from a single fold to five-fold puzzles.

Daily fold

One fresh sheet a day, the same for everyone, gentler early in the week and trickier by the weekend.

Free fold

An endless run of sheets. Fold them tidily, or let three crumples end the run.

Album and desk paper

Every picture you reveal joins your album. Earn paper cranes and change the colour of your desk.

Quiet by design.

Works offline, no account, calm motion switch, soft paper sounds and haptics you can turn off. Made for portrait and landscape, phones and tablets.
