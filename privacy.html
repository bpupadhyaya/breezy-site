Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026. This policy covers Tinyhive, Creaselet, Tilebrook, Beamling, Arrowlark and other mobile games from Breezy, a games brand of EqualInformation LLC ("we", "us"). It explains what the games do with information. Questions: support@equalinformation.com.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-up. Your progress (levels, stars, collections and settings) stays on your device.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Each Breezy game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Showing ads involves an advertising identifier and a little usage data, described below.
  • You can say no to personalised ads, and you can remove forced ads with a one-time purchase.

What stays on your device

Game progress, scores, stars, rewards you have earned, collections and stickers, your chosen look and settings (sound, vibration, calm mode) are saved locally in the app. We do not receive them. If you delete the app, they are removed with it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

To pay for the games, each Breezy game shows ads served by Google AdMob: optional videos you choose to watch (for a hint or an extra try) and a short ad after some finished levels. To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use the following from your device:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iPhone, only if you allow tracking);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system version, language and app version; and
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown or tapped.

We do not receive this data about you and we do not sell it. Google's own policies describe how it handles it: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Consent form. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent form before ads, with equal choices to consent or not. In some US states it shows a form to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. You can change your answer later where the game offers a privacy option.
  • iPhone. The game asks for Apple's "Allow tracking" permission after you finish your first level. If you decline, ads are not personalised using the IDFA. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Settings > Privacy (or Google > Ads).
  • Remove forced ads. A one-time in-app purchase removes ads that appear on their own. Optional videos stay optional.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment card or bank details. The store tells the app whether the purchase was made so the game can unlock it.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, write to us and we will help.

Retention and deletion

We keep no personal data about you on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Local game data is removed when you uninstall the app or use Reset progress in Settings. Data held by Google or the app stores is governed by their policies and controls.

Security and changes

Because we hold no accounts or personal data, there is little to secure on our side; the game connects to the internet only to show ads and process store purchases. If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for material changes we will say so in the app or on this page.
