Privacy

Status: this page describes the current Tinyhive test build. It will be updated before any release that includes ads or purchases.

What the game stores

The current build keeps your progress (levels, stars, honey, settings and themes) on your own device. It does not create an account and does not send this data to Breezy.

Ads and purchases

Tinyhive is planned to be free with optional rewarded videos and occasional ads between finished puzzles, plus a one-time purchase to remove forced ads. When those features go live, ad and store partners may process device identifiers and usage data as described in their own policies. This page will list those partners, what they collect and how you can opt out before that release.

Children

Tinyhive is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.
